What is Surfmate?

Surfmate is the social platform for surfers. Log your sessions, discover surf events, find surfers around you, and build your surf community — all in one place.

  • 🌊 Log your sessions
  • 🤝 Connect with surfers
  • 📍 Discover surf spots & events
  • 📖 Build your surf story

What does a Surfmate Creator do?

You don't need to become a full-time influencer. We simply want you to use Surfmate as part of your normal surf life and share your experience with your community.

  1. Use Surfmate

    Download the app and start logging your sessions.

  2. Share your experience

    Show your community how you actually use Surfmate.

  3. Bring surfers together

    Share your personal Creator Link with surfers who might love Surfmate too.

  4. Get rewarded

    Earn a performance bonus for every new surfer you bring to Surfmate who logs their first session.

Your Creator Journey

  1. Step 01

    Try Surfmate

    Use Surfmate for your own sessions. We want you to actually know and enjoy the app before recommending it.

  2. Step 02

    Log your sessions

    Log at least 2–3 surf sessions and explore the app.

  3. Step 03

    Share it

    Share Surfmate naturally through your Instagram Stories and show your community how you use it.

  4. Step 04

    Invite your surfers

    Get your personal Surfmate Creator Link and share it with your community.

  5. Step 05

    Earn

    Every new referred user who logs their first session earns you a performance bonus.

What can you share?

  • Your actual session

    “Just logged today's session on Surfmate 🌊”

  • Your surf history

    “I've started keeping track of all my sessions here.”

  • Finding surfers

    “You can actually find surfers around you who are open to connecting.”

  • Events

    “Found this surf event on Surfmate 👀”

  • Your recommendation

    “If you're a surfer, you should check out Surfmate.”

Keep it natural. We don't want ads or influencer speak — just your real surf life.
