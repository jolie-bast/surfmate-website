When I was 18, I fell in love with surfing. But I lived in Hamburg, a city without any surf community. For three years I had no surf friends, so I paddled out alone with my Decathlon 8'0 softtop, often at the wrong spots, with no one to ask and no one to share the stoke with.

My surf journey got lost in camera rolls, stories and memories I couldn’t hold on to.

That’s why Surfmate exists: so no surfer has to start alone. A place to find surfers near you, help each other out and capture every session of your surf story.

What started as one surfer’s story is now a community of more than 2,000 surfers. Now it’s yours. 🌊
