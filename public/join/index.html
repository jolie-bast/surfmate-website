The project

Surfmate connects surfers worldwide.

In the water, you don’t need anyone. Most of us love an empty lineup. But out of the water, we want surf friends who share our passion. For beginners and intermediates especially, finding a way into the scene is tough.

That’s where we come in. We bring surfers together, especially where surf is rare, show them events and places to connect, and in the long run, create our own.

We want to fight loneliness in surfing and do some good without changing what surfing is. We start with wave riding, followed by rapid surfing, then kitesurfing, windsurfing, wing foiling, and more.

This is not a fixed job.

It’s an invitation to join a project we care about. Bring your expertise, grow with us, and help build something that moves the surf scene. The long-term goal is to found Surfmate and create real jobs in surfing. People who are here from the start help shape that.

  • You join a project, not a fixed position
  • A short email is enough — no cover letter, no CV
  • If it fits, we onboard you and you start with your ideas

What you get

  1. Insights into building a global surf community brand

    Work with us on exciting projects, grow in the areas you’re genuinely interested in, and join us at events.

  2. Hands-on experience for your CV

    Gain real experience on an innovative project that you can actually show, with concrete results instead of just a line on your résumé.

  3. Shape it, don’t just do it

    You’re part of a committed team that brings creative ideas to life and shapes its own structures. It’s not about ticking off tasks, it’s about taking ownership and growing together.

  4. A crew that surfs together

    Team get-togethers with surfing, Surfmate Wear, and events we head to as a crew. Because that’s what Surfmate is all about: surfing together, not alone.

  5. Network & community

    Work alongside creative minds and build connections with NGOs, initiatives, and partners that open new doors for you.

  6. Make a positive impact

    Become part of a global surf network and make a real difference in the surf scene. Your work helps connect surfers and fight loneliness in a sport that can be pretty challenging.

The team

What we decide together

At Surfmate you’re not only there for your own tasks. You help shape it. We decide these things together as a team:

  • Session of the Month

    Which session from the community we celebrate and feature.

  • Community Challenges

    Which challenges we run, and what people can win.

  • Surfmate Wear

    How our clothing brand evolves, from new designs to the next collection.

  • Collaborations

    Which brands, creators, and partners we work with.

  • Events

    Which contests, festivals, and surf events we go to.

  • Team get-together

    When and where we meet as a team to surf, this winter or next year.

Get in touch

How to join

Send a short email to jolie@surfmate.eu with four things, or write us a DM on Instagram and tell us what you’re into.

  1. The role you’re interested in
  2. A few sentences about you and your relationship with surfing
  3. Why you want to join us
  4. A link to your portfolio, GitHub, Instagram, or LinkedIn, if you have one

No cover letter, no CV. After that we meet on a short call, and if it fits, we onboard you so you know what’s going on. You join the team and get started with your ideas.
