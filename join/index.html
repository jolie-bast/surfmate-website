The project

Surfmate connects surfers worldwide.

We want to fight loneliness in surfing, bring people together in the lineup and on land, and do some good without changing what surfing is. We start with wave riding. Rapid surfing comes next, then kitesurfing, windsurfing, wing foiling, and more.

This is not a fixed job.

It’s an invitation to join a project we care about. Bring your expertise, grow with us, and help build something that moves the surf scene. The long-term goal is to found Surfmate and create real jobs in surfing. People who are here from the start help shape that.

  • You join a project, not a fixed position
  • A short email is enough — no cover letter, no CV
  • If it fits, you start with a small project together

How to join

Send a short email to jolie@surfmate.eu with four things:

  1. The role you’re interested in
  2. A few sentences about you and your relationship with surfing
  3. Why you want to join us
  4. A link to your portfolio, GitHub, Instagram, or LinkedIn, if you have one

No cover letter, no CV. After that we meet on a short call, and if it fits, you start with a small project together.
